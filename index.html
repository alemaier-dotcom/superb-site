About our business

What we do

Superb Service LLC is a business development and technology solutions design firm serving technology consulting firms. We identify prospective clients with technology needs, scope and design the solution, and match each opportunity with the technology consulting firm best suited to deliver it.

We also provide delivery supervision for the length of the engagement.
